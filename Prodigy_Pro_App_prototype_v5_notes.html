The “risk” vocabulary

The aim is to describe the same thing in words that invite action instead of worry, without hiding anything. Each change keeps the meaning; it just leads with what the investor gains.

What we deliberately kept

  • Fund riskometer and “Risk” labels on fund pages, compare and baskets. These follow SEBI’s riskometer format and must read “risk”.
  • “Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks …” is a mandatory disclaimer.
  • SEBI risk category (e.g. “Moderately aggressive”) still shows under the profile name and must stay in the advice record. The friendly name is a display label only.
  • The underlying numbers, thresholds and recommendations are unchanged. Only wording and layout moved.

Please have compliance sign off the persona names. Some regulators expect the risk category to be shown clearly to the client; v5 shows it right below the name.

Things to decide

  1. Persona names. Are “Safety first → Bold grower” the right tone for BFC? An alternative set: Protector, Planner, Balancer, Builder, Achiever.
  2. “Market exposure”. We replaced “portfolio risk score” with this. An option is “Growth level”, which sounds more positive but could be read as a promise of returns.
  3. Health page length. It is now about two-thirds of v4. If it should be shorter still, “Where your gains came from” and “Company size” could move into a “See more insights” expander.
  4. “Doing well” vs “Satisfactory”. The RIAs used “Satisfactory”; “Doing well” is warmer. Confirm with them.
